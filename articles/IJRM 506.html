PERCEPTION OF GEN Z TOWARDS SUSTAINABLE CONSUMPTION AND LIFESTYLE CHOICES IN INDIA

Dr. Neha Goyal, Vishakha Bharti

Abstract

This study examines Generation Z's awareness, attitudes, and behaviours concerning sustainable consumption and lifestyle choices in India. Data were collected using a structured five-point Likert-scale questionnaire distributed through Google Forms. The findings indicate generally positive attitudes and knowledge regarding sustainability, along with a willingness to reduce waste and use environmentally friendly products. The study highlights the need for awareness programmes and policy support to encourage sustainable consumption and lifestyle practices among young consumers.
